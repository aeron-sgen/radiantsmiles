## §3 — Schema requirements (net-new; nothing exists today)

The baseline carries only generic `Organization`, `WebSite`, `WebPage` and `BreadcrumbList` markup. For a seven-office practice this is the single largest local-SEO gap. Required at build:

- **`Dentist` (LocalBusiness) on each of the 7 location pages** — name, image, address, geo, telephone, openingHoursSpecification from `client-rules.json` contactData, areaServed, and `sameAs` once the owner supplies profile URLs.
- **`MedicalProcedure` or `Service`** on each of the 28 service leaves, linked to the parent organisation.
- **`Physician`** on each of the 10 clinician pages, with `memberOf` pointing at the practice.
- **`FAQPage`** on `/about/faq/`.
- **`AggregateRating`** on `/about/reviews/` — **only** once the owner nominates the platform of record, and published with its scope stated per `content-bible.md` §6. Do not aggregate across offices.
- **`BreadcrumbList`** site-wide, reflecting the new parent hierarchy.
