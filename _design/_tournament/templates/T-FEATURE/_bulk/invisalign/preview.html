Invisalign in Las Vegas and Henderson

Clear removable aligners that straighten teeth without a mouth full of metal, planned and reviewed by our own dentists.

Book an appointment Call the office

Plenty of adults want their teeth straighter and put it off anyway.

The picture in their head is brackets, wires and looking fifteen again in a work meeting.

So it waits. Meanwhile the crowded spot stays hard to floss. The tooth that sits out of line keeps taking more load than it should.

Aligners change the trade. They are clear, they come out for meals, and you can brush and floss the way you already do.

The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 21% of adults aged 19 to 64 have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. The same source records 55% for adults aged 65 and older, and finds that dental visits track coverage directly.

Take them out to eat.

No list of foods to avoid.

The aligner comes out for the meal and goes back in afterwards.

Keep your cleaning routine.

There are no brackets to floss around.

You brush and floss the teeth themselves, exactly as you do now.

Wear something that is not obvious.

The aligners are clear plastic rather than metal brackets, and they come out when you want them out.

We take records first.

Photographs, digital X-rays and a record of how your bite fits together.

Nothing is planned before we can see how the teeth actually meet.

We tell you if aligners suit your case.

Not every bite does.

If yours is better treated another way, we will say so rather than sell you the tray.

You wear them in sequence.

Each aligner moves the teeth a small amount, then you change to the next one on the schedule you are given.

Our own Invisalign page states that 20 to 29 sets per arch is typical, and that the trays come out for eating and drinking. Captured 30 July 2026.

We check the movement as it goes.

Review appointments compare what has happened against what was planned, and the plan is adjusted where it needs to be.

Retention follows the movement.

Teeth drift back without a retainer worn as directed.

Ask us what retention your quote includes before you start.

Orthodontics is one of the six things we do in-house, alongside general, cosmetic, restorative, surgical and periodontal care.

Seven offices, one practice, one roster.

Our roster includes Dr. Karthikeyan Subramani, who holds an MSc in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics. He also holds the Milo Hellman Award from the American Association of Orthodontists, 2019, per our own doctors page, fetched 30 July 2026.

That is a structural point rather than a comparison. A practice that refers orthodontics out cannot keep the planning and the general care in one practice.

Our Lake Mead office holds 4.5 stars across 506 patient reviews on Birdeye, fetched 30 July 2026.

That is the record for one office, not a figure for all seven.

What does Invisalign cost?

We quote after records are taken, because the fee follows the number of aligners your case needs. We do not publish treatment prices, and we will not guess at one.

Will my insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan and on how orthodontic care is coded. Network status varies by office and by plan.

Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

Each new aligner feels tight going in, because that is the tray moving the teeth. There are no brackets or wires being tightened, and no needle is involved.
