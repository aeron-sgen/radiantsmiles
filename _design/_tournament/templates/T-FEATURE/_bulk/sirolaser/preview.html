SIROlaser Gum Treatment in Las Vegas

Laser assisted periodontal treatment using the SIROlaser, carried out at our own offices by our own clinicians.

Book an appointment Call the office

Decide with the device named.

You can look the SIROlaser up yourself before you agree to any gum treatment.

Start with a measurement, not a quote.

Our own scaling and root planing page says the depth of the gum pockets is assessed before treatment.

Finish the course without being sent across town.

Examination, cleaning below the gumline and laser assisted treatment all happen inside this practice.

When people hear the words gum surgery, they picture a scalpel.

Cutting, stitches, swelling and time off work.

That picture is why gum treatment gets postponed. The appointment feels like something to brace for rather than book.

So the pocket stays. The infection keeps working under the gumline while the decision waits for a better time.

We would rather tell you exactly which instrument we use, and let you decide with the facts in front of you.

Cost stacks on top of the dread. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly.

Our Lake Mead office is rated 4.5 stars across 506 patient reviews on Birdeye, fetched 30 July 2026.

That number covers that office alone.

Here it is the same practice throughout.

  1. 01

    We examine and measure first.

    A clinician assesses the depth of the gum pockets. Our own digital X-rays page says digital X-rays may reveal bone loss. That assessment decides what follows.

  2. 02

    We clean below the gumline.

    Laser assisted treatment sits alongside scaling and root planing. It is part of a course of periodontal care, not a substitute for it.

  3. 03

    We use the SIROlaser where it is indicated.

    Our own page says the option may not be right for some patients, so your case is reviewed first. The device is named because it can be verified.

  4. 04

    We examine the gums again afterwards.

    Our own scaling and root planing page says the next appointment checks how well the pockets have healed. If they still measure more than 3mm, that page says more intensive treatment may be recommended.

A practice that does not deliver periodontics itself has to send this work elsewhere. That is what the referral model means for a patient.

Periodontics is one of six specialties delivered in-house across our seven offices in Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson.

Where offices are separately owned or separately branded, the same equipment and the same clinician cannot be promised at each one. That is a limit of the model, not a swipe at anyone.

Our clinicians are named on the site with their credentials. Both are recorded on our own team page, fetched 30 July 2026, with the other eight dentists on the roster.

What does laser assisted gum treatment cost?

We quote once your gums have been examined, because the number of sites treated drives the work. We do not publish treatment prices.

Does insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan and on how the treatment is coded. Our insurance and financing page publishes the full list of named plans. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

The area is numbed where numbing is needed. We will not tell you the procedure is painless, and we will not promise a recovery time we have not published.

Is a laser better than surgery?

That is a clinical judgement for your specific pockets, not a slogan. We will explain why one is being recommended for you, and what the alternative is.

New patients can start with our $45 exam and X-ray, or $29 if you are a senior.
