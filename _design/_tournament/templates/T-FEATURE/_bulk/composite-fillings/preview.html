Laugh without a flash of metal.

The repair is shaded to the tooth beside it, so it is not the first thing people see.

Keep more of your own tooth.

Composite bonds to the tooth structure, so the preparation can stay conservative rather than being cut wide to hold a filling in.

Deal with it instead of watching it.

The decayed part is removed and the tooth is rebuilt, rather than left to get wider.

A cavity does not heal. It is a hole in the enamel that gets wider and deeper while you carry on eating. Early on it is a small repair.

Left long enough, decay reaches the nerve. At that point the conversation is about root canal treatment or an extraction, not a filling. Same tooth, much bigger job.

The other thing people mention is how a repair looks. A metal filling shows every time you laugh. A composite is shaded to the tooth it sits in.

  1. 01

    We find it.

    Decay between teeth and under an old filling is not visible to you. Digital X-rays show what a mirror cannot.

  2. 02

    We remove the decay.

    The softened part of the tooth is taken out first.

  3. 03

    We rebuild the tooth.

    Composite is placed in layers, cured, then shaped back to the form of the tooth. The shade is matched to the tooth next to it.

  4. 04

    We check the bite.

    You are asked to bite down and say how it feels. A filling that sits high is adjusted before you leave the chair.

What does a filling cost?

We quote after we have looked, because the price follows the size and the surface being repaired. We do not publish treatment prices on this site.

Does insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan. Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife and the Culinary Health Fund are among the carriers listed on our insurance and financing page. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

Local anaesthetic is used where it is needed, and you are told before it is given. If you feel anything during the repair, raise a hand and we stop.

New patients can start with the $45 exam and X-ray, or $29 for seniors.

Both prices are published on our own site and were current on 30 July 2026.
