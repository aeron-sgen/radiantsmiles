Scaling and Root Planing in Las Vegas

A deep clean below the gumline that takes tartar off the root, so the gum has a clean surface to reattach to.

Book an appointment Call the office

Plaque left on the tooth for some time turns hard. Once it has set under the gum, no amount of brushing will shift it, because the brush cannot reach the root surface.

What happens next is quiet. The gum lifts slightly away from the tooth, a pocket forms, and deeper pockets tend to house more bacteria. Bleeding when you brush is one of the symptoms our own periodontal disease page lists.

Left alone, that pocket can get deeper rather than shallower. Bone around the tooth is what pays for it. Our own bone grafting page says periodontal disease can permanently damage the jawbone that supports the teeth.

Money is a large part of why this waits. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly.

We examine before we treat.

Our own page says these treatments follow a thorough examination of the mouth. The dentist takes X-rays, conducts a visual examination and makes a diagnosis first. Our own X-ray page says digital X-rays may reveal bone loss.

We numb the area where it is needed.

Our own page says local anaesthetic may be used. That depends on the gums, the tartar, the pocket depth and the progression of the periodontitis. It is a thorough clean of a surface that is normally covered, so comfort matters.

We scale and smooth the root.

Tartar comes off below the gumline, and the root surface is smoothed, because clean smooth roots help bacteria colonise less easily.

We check the gums again later.

Our own page says the gums are examined again to see how well the pockets have healed. If they still measure more than 3mm, more intensive treatment may be recommended.

Stop treating bleeding gums as normal.

Once the root is clean, the gum has a fair chance to settle down and stay attached.

Hold on to the teeth you have.

Gum treatment is about the foundation, not the crown. Keeping the bone is what keeps the tooth in place.

What does scaling and root planing cost?

It is quoted after an examination, because the fee depends on how much of the mouth is involved. We do not publish treatment prices.

Will my insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan. Our insurance and financing page publishes the full list of named plans. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

Local anaesthetic may be used, depending on the gums, the tartar, the pocket depth and the disease progression. Gums can be tender afterwards, and teeth can feel sensitive. We will not promise a recovery time we have not published.
