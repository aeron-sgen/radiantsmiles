Root Canal Therapy in Las Vegas

Treatment that removes the infection inside a tooth, so the tooth can stay where it is. Same-day and emergency appointments are available.

Book an appointment Call the office

Deal with the source of the pain.

The treatment removes the infected tissue from inside the tooth. That is the thing generating the pressure.

Keep your own tooth in your own jaw.

A treated tooth stays in place and keeps doing its job. An extraction is followed by a discussion about replacement choices such as a bridge, a denture or a dental implant.

Be seen while it is still today.

Same-day and emergency appointments are available at our offices. Call the office nearest you and say that it is painful.

The nerve inside a tooth sits in a sealed space. When it becomes infected, the pressure has nowhere to go. That is why the pain can be severe.

Pain that throbs or keeps you awake is a reason to ask for an urgent appointment.

It does not settle by itself. It can quieten, which people read as recovery. The infection is still there when it comes back.

By then the choice may be narrower. Treating the tooth early is a different conversation from treating it after an abscess.

Cost is part of why people ride it out. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly.

We find out what is actually happening.

A clinician examines the tooth and tests it. Digital X-rays may reveal decay between the teeth and problems inside a tooth or below the gum line. Not every bad toothache is a root canal, and you get told which one yours is.

We numb it properly first.

Nothing starts until the tooth is numb. If you can still feel it, say so, and we add more before beginning.

We open the tooth and clean the canals.

The infected tissue is removed and the inside of the tooth is cleaned out and shaped.

We seal it.

The cleaned canals are filled and the access is closed.

Our Lake Mead office is rated 4.5 stars across 506 patient reviews on Birdeye, fetched 30 July 2026.

Root canal treatment is done by our own clinicians. We are one dental group running seven offices across Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson.

Care is delivered in-house, not passed to a separate business. That matters most when a tooth is hurting. The office that diagnoses it can treat it, and the crown that follows is arranged inside the same practice.

Where treatment is referred out, it is arranged with a separate business rather than inside one practice.

Our clinicians are named on the site, with their qualifications, so you can read who they are before you book. Booking is by office rather than by clinician, so a booking does not select which of them you see.

We rebuild the top of the tooth.

Our crowns page lists a tooth that has had a root canal as one of the reasons for a crown. That is planned with you, not sprung on you.

What does a root canal cost?

It is quoted after the examination, because the tooth and the number of canals decide the work. We do not publish treatment prices. Any figure given before the X-ray would only change after it.

Does insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan, and on how the treatment and the crown are coded. Our insurance and financing page publishes the full list of named plans. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

We will not tell you the treatment is painless. The tooth is numbed before anything starts. If you feel anything sharp during it, tell us and we stop.

What happens if I wait?

The infection does not resolve on its own, and the options can narrow over time. If you are in pain now, call the nearest office rather than sitting with it.

If you are new to us, the exam and X-ray is $45, or $29 for seniors.

Both prices are published on our own site and were current on 30 July 2026.
