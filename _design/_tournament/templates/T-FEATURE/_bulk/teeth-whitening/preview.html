Teeth Whitening in Las Vegas

Professional whitening that reaches past surface stain, at seven offices across Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson.

Book an appointment Call the office

If you have tried a pharmacy kit, you know the pattern. Strips and pens lift some of the surface stain, and then they stop. That plateau is where a kit reaches its limit.

Patchiness is the other limit. A flat strip sits against an uneven arch, so the front surfaces lighten and the edges do not.

Stain also sits in two places. Some of it is on the surface. Some of it is deeper in the tooth, where a pharmacy kit cannot reach.

Coverage is worth being plain about. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly. Those figures describe dental coverage and dental visits generally, not whitening.

  1. 01

    We examine before we whiten.

    A clinician checks for decay, cracks and gum recession first. Digital X-rays are taken where they add something. Whitening over an untreated cavity is not a plan.

  2. 02

    We point out the work already in your mouth.

    Existing crowns, veneers and fillings are identified at the examination, so the plan accounts for them. Crowns, veneers and fillings do not change shade at all. That conversation happens before treatment, not after.

  3. 03

    We fit the trays, then you whiten at home.

    This usually takes two visits. Impressions are taken at the first, to make custom clear plastic trays. At the second you try the trays for fit, and adjustments are made if they are needed.

  4. 04

    We tell you what to expect afterwards.

    The trays are worn with a whitening solution. How long depends on how stained your teeth are and how light you want them. If your teeth feel sensitive, tell us rather than waiting it out.

Our own FAQ page publishes an in-office route too.

It states the treatment may need more than one visit, each lasting 30 to 60 minutes, captured 30 July 2026. The gums are protected while a bleaching solution is applied, and a special light may be used.

Our own SIROlaser page also lists teeth whitening among that device's applications, captured the same day. Ask at the examination which route is open to you.

Stop editing your photographs.

A supervised result covers the whole visible arch, not only the flat surfaces a strip can reach.

Know the plan before you agree to it.

A clinician examines your teeth first, and the treatment is quoted after that examination, not before.

Plan around the work you already have.

Crowns, veneers and fillings hold their shade. We tell you that before treatment, not after it.

Your whitening is planned and fitted by the same practice that examined you. We are one dental group running seven offices across Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson. We are not a franchise and not a referral network.

That matters here for a plain reason. Where offices are separately owned, a consistent standard between them cannot be promised. This is one practice with one clinical roster across all seven offices.

Our named clinicians are listed on the site, with their qualifications. Booking is by office rather than by clinician, so a booking does not select which of them you see.

Our Lake Mead office holds 4.5 stars across 506 patient reviews on Birdeye, fetched 30 July 2026.

That figure is the record for that one office.

What does whitening cost?

We quote treatment after an examination, because the plan depends on what your teeth are actually doing. We do not publish treatment prices on this site.

Does insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan. Our insurance and financing page publishes the full list of named plans. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

There is no drilling and no injection. Some patients may experience tooth sensitivity afterwards, as our own FAQ page states. Tell us if that happens, because there are things we can do about it.
