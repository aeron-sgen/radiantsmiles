Dental Onlays in Las Vegas

An onlay rebuilds the broken cusp of a tooth and leaves the sound part of it where it is.

Book an appointment Call the office

A cusp is one of the raised points on the chewing surface of a back tooth.

They take the load, so they are what tends to snap. It can happen on something ordinary, like bread.

An onlay covers and rebuilds the cusp instead.

When a cusp goes, the reflex answer is a full crown.

A crown works, but the sound part of the tooth has to be cut down to make room for it. You lose good structure to fix a broken piece.

It is the option in the middle, and this is the case it exists for.

Get back to eating on that side.

The onlay is bonded in place and adjusted to your bite. Chewing should stop being something you plan around.

Keep the healthy part of the tooth.

The onlay covers the damaged cusp. The rest of your tooth is left alone rather than reduced for a crown.

See the choice before it is made.

We show you the crack and the digital X-ray. Filling, onlay and crown each cost a different amount of tooth, and you get told which is which.

  1. 01

    We find out how far the crack goes.

    A clinician examines the cusp and the surrounding tooth. Digital X-rays are taken where they add something. A crack that reaches the root changes the plan, and you are told if it does.

  2. 02

    We remove what has failed.

    The broken material and any decay under it come out. What is left gets shaped so the onlay can sit over the cusp and grip properly.

  3. 03

    The onlay is made to your tooth.

    It is made to an impression of the prepared tooth. It is not a stock part adjusted to fit.

  4. 04

    We seat it, bond it and balance the bite.

    You bite down, and a few adjustments may be necessary before your bite is comfortable. That step is worth doing slowly, because a high onlay is what cracks next.

  5. 05

    We watch the tooth after that.

    Repaired cusps get checked at recall, along with the bite that broke the first one.

The clinician who assesses the cusp is the clinician who restores it.

We are one dental group running seven offices across Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson.

One practice, one clinical roster.

That is the practical difference here. If the onlay needs adjusting, you are back with the practice that placed it rather than starting again elsewhere.

The same holds if the tooth beside it starts to go the same way. There is no referral network in between and no separately owned office.

Why not just do the crown?

Sometimes a crown is the right call, and we will say so. An onlay is offered when enough sound tooth remains to be worth keeping. The decision is made at the tooth, not in advance.

What does an onlay cost?

We quote it once we have seen how much of the tooth is gone. We do not publish treatment prices. A figure quoted before the examination would only change after it.

Does insurance pay for it?

Coverage depends on your plan and on how the restoration is coded. Named plans are listed in full on our insurance and financing page. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

The tooth is numbed before the preparation starts. Pressure and vibration can still be felt while we work, because numbing does not remove those. If anything feels sharp, say so and we stop.
