Prophylaxis Teeth Cleaning in Las Vegas

A professional cleaning that lifts the plaque and hardened tartar a toothbrush cannot reach.

Book an appointment Call the office

Plaque is soft at first. Left on the tooth for some time, it hardens into tartar, and from then on only an instrument will shift it.

That is the honest limit of brushing well. A good routine keeps new plaque down; it does not remove what has already set along the gumline or between the back teeth.

Our own prophylaxis page puts cleaning below the gumline inside this same procedure. We also publish scaling and root planing as a treatment of its own. Which one your mouth needs is decided at the examination, not before it.

Leave with a clean surface.

Tartar comes off, the tooth feels smooth again, and new plaque has less to cling to.

Find the small things while they are small.

A cleaning visit is also a look. A chip, a leaking edge or an early cavity is easier to deal with now.

Learn where you are actually missing.

We show you the spots the brush keeps skipping.

  1. 01

    We look before we clean.

    A clinician checks the teeth, the gums and any existing work. Digital X-rays are taken where they show something an examination cannot.

  2. 02

    We remove the deposit.

    Tartar is taken off above the gumline with scaling tools. Our own page puts removal of calculus from the gum pockets in this same procedure, captured 30 July 2026. Stain can come off with it, which is why teeth can look brighter afterwards.

  1. 03

    We polish and finish.

    The surfaces are smoothed so there is less for plaque to grip. You are told which areas needed the most work. Our own page says recommendations can be provided for altering your home care routine.

  2. 04

    We agree when to do it again.

    Our own page recommends prophylaxis twice annually as a preventative measure. For periodontitis sufferers it says every 3 to 4 months, captured 30 July 2026. You leave knowing which applies to you and why.

What does a cleaning cost?

We do not publish treatment prices. Note that the published new patient offer covers an examination and X-ray, so a cleaning is quoted separately after we have looked.

Will my insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan. Our insurance and financing page publishes the full list of named plans. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

Our own page says a prophylaxis can be done during a regular dental visit. Where severe periodontal disease is suspected, general anaesthetic may be used, fetched 30 July 2026. Tell us at the start if an area feels sore. Ask your dentist anything you are unsure of.
