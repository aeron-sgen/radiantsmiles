Fluoride Treatment in Las Vegas

A professional fluoride application that helps strengthen enamel, for children and for adults.

Book an appointment Call the office

Enamel weakens long before a cavity is visible.

The surface loses minerals first, and nothing about that stage looks or feels like a problem.

You cannot see it in a mirror. It is the quiet part of decay, the months or years before there is a hole to fill.

Fluoride is aimed at that window. It is not a cure for a cavity, and we will not describe it as one. It is help for a surface that is losing ground.

The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly in that same source.

In 2023, 53% of privately insured adults had a visit, against 16% of those with no dental insurance.

Prevention only counts if somebody keeps looking for the change.

Fluoride is applied by the same practice that examined you, at the same visit where the need was found.

We check whether you need it.

Your history of decay, your sensitivity, your medications and a dry mouth all change the answer.

The fluoride is applied to the teeth.

There is no drilling, no injection and no numbing involved.

You get after-care instructions before you leave.

If there is a wait before eating or drinking, we tell you what it is at the chair.

Give a weak spot a chance to hold.

Early enamel loss is the stage where a small step is still worth taking.

Do something for sensitivity you already have.

Exposed and sensitive tooth roots are one of the reasons a dentist may recommend fluoride. Our own fluoride page states that remineralizing the root can reduce sensitivity.

Cover the whole family in one place.

Children and adults are both seen at all seven of our offices, so nobody gets sent elsewhere for this.

We run seven offices across Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson as one group.

One clinical roster covers all seven offices, so the next visit is with the same clinical team.

We are not a referral network, and our offices are not separately owned or separately branded.

Our Lake Mead office holds 4.5 stars across 506 patient reviews on Birdeye, fetched 30 July 2026.

What does fluoride treatment cost?

We quote after an examination, because whether you need it at all depends on what we find. We do not publish treatment prices.

Does insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan. Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife and the Culinary Health Fund are among the 41 carriers listed on our insurance and financing page. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt, and is it only for children?

There is no needle and nothing is drilled, and it is not only for children. Our own fluoride page publishes that dentists may recommend it for children and for adult patients, captured 30 July 2026.

If you are new to us, the exam and X-ray are $45, and $29 for seniors.

Both prices are published on our own site and were current on 30 July 2026.

Payment plans are available through Care Credit, Cherry, Sunbit and Lending Club. Terms are set by the lender and are subject to credit approval. They are not terms offered by this practice.
