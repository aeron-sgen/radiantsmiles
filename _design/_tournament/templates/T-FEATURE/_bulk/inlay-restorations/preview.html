Dental Inlays in Las Vegas

A custom inlay repairs damage too large for a filling, without cutting the tooth down for a crown.

Book an appointment Call the office

A filling is the right answer until the cavity gets wide.

Once the hole is large, what surrounds it is a thin rim of tooth around a big plug of material. That rim flexes every time you chew.

Cracks start there. The crack stays silent until the day a cusp gives way on something soft. The repair that was small has become a crown.

That is what waiting does to a back tooth.

Cost is part of why people wait. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. The same source reports that dental visits track coverage directly.

Keep more of your own tooth.

An inlay sits inside the cusps of the tooth. A crown covers the whole tooth, so more healthy structure has to come off first.

Chew on that side again.

The inlay is bonded into the prepared space and shaped to your bite. The aim is a tooth you stop noticing.

Decide with the picture in front of you.

We show you the tooth and the digital X-ray. You choose between a filling, an inlay and a crown knowing what each one costs in tooth structure.

  1. 01

    We look, and we image.

    A clinician examines the tooth and any existing filling. Digital X-rays are taken where they show something the eye cannot. You are told what is there.

  2. 02

    We prepare the space.

    Any decay or old filling material comes out. What is left is shaped so the inlay can seat cleanly, with the walls kept as thick as they can be.

  3. 03

    The inlay is made to that space.

    It is made to an impression of your prepared tooth. It is not selected from a tray of stock sizes.

  4. 04

    We fit it, bond it and check the bite.

    You bite down, and a few adjustments may be necessary before your bite is comfortable. A high spot is quick to correct in the chair and unpleasant to live with.

  5. 05

    We check it again later.

    Bonded work gets looked at at every recall visit, the same as everything else in your mouth.

The tooth is prepared and the inlay is fitted by the same practice.

We are one dental group running seven offices across Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson.

You are not handed to a separate business to finish the work.

That matters when a restoration later needs a small adjustment. You come back to the practice that did the work, not to a separate business you have to chase. There is no referral network in between, and no separately branded group to find.

Our Lake Mead office is rated 4.5 stars across 506 patient reviews on Birdeye, fetched 30 July 2026.

What does an inlay cost?

We quote it after we have seen the tooth, because the size of the damage decides the work. We do not publish treatment prices. A number given before an examination would only change afterwards.

Will my insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan and on how the restoration is coded. Named plans are listed in full on our insurance and financing page. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

The tooth is numbed before any preparation begins. You may feel pressure rather than pain. Tell us if anything feels sharp and we stop and add more anaesthetic.

How many visits does it take?

Our own inlay pages publish two appointments as the usual count. The first appointment takes the impressions and places a temporary filling. The second cements the finished inlay and checks the bite.

New patients can start with our $45 exam and X-ray, or $29 if you are a senior.

Both prices are published on our own site and were current on 30 July 2026.
