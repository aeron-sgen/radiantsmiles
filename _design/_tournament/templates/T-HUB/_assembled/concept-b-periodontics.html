All seven offices

  • Gum disease.

    Bleeding, swelling or gums pulling away from the tooth. Find out how far it has gone, and what stops it.

  • Deep cleaning.

    Scaling and root planing to clean below the gumline, where a normal cleaning cannot reach.

  • Gum recession.

    When the gum pulls back and the root starts to show or feel sensitive.

  • Laser treatment.

    The SIROlaser, used for gum work where it is the right tool.

  • Crown lengthening.

    Reshaping gum and bone so a crown or filling has enough tooth to hold on to.

  • Antibiotic treatment.

    Targeted antibiotic therapy alongside cleaning, where the infection calls for it.

  • Grinding and clenching.

    Night guards for bruxism, made to your own bite and reviewed over time.

  • Oral cancer screening.

    A short visual and physical check, done as part of your exam.

  • Routine cleaning.

    Standard prophylaxis for gums that are healthy and staying that way.

Gum problems are quiet. They rarely hurt early, which is why they are usually found at a checkup rather than reported by the patient.

Where a practice refers out, you are sent somewhere else. You see a general dentist, then a specialist across town, which means a new practice, a new front desk and a new wait.

We treat this in house, at our own offices, with named clinicians. The person who checks your gums next works in the same practice, not a separate business across town.
