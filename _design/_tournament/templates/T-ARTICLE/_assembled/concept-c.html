Credentials

Dr. Adrian Ruiz is a second generation dentist. He earned his Doctorate in Dental Surgery at the UCLA School of Dentistry in 1995, and has over 22 years of practice experience.

Those details come from his own biography on the practice site, fetched 30 July 2026. You can check them there before you book.

You are choosing a named clinician with a stated qualification and a stated year, not an anonymous appointment slot.

Approach

The appointment runs in plain language. You are told what the digital X-ray shows, what it means, and what your choices are.

Where a finding can wait, you are told that it can wait. Where it cannot, you are told what happens if it is left.

Nothing here rates his ability or his manner. We cannot source a claim like that for you, so we do not make one.
