Credentials

Dr. Stephanie Andrade holds a Doctor of Dental Medicine from the UNLV School of Dental Medicine. She is fluent in Spanish.

Her stated special interest is cosmetic and restorative dentistry, including veneers, crowns and bridges.

Each of those details is carried from her biography on the practice site, fetched 30 July 2026.

One other dentist on our team is recorded as fluent in Spanish, Dr. Andy Landaverde.

Approach

A consultation in your own language is not a courtesy. It is how informed consent actually works.

Dr. Andrade speaks Spanish, so that conversation happens directly, without a relative translating the clinical part.

Where they practise

Dr. Andrade sees patients within the Radiant Smiles group.

Our seven offices cover Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson.
