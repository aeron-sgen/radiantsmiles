Dr. Karthikeyan Subramani

An orthodontist with Radiant Smiles. Orthodontic care is delivered in-house, by our own team, at our own offices.

Three dated qualifications

He completed a Fellowship in Orthodontics and Craniofacial Research at the University of Kentucky in 2012.

He earned an MSc in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics at the same institution in 2015.

the Milo Hellman Award from the American Association of Orthodontists 2019

That is three dated qualifications and two dated awards. Every one of them names the body behind it.

You can check each before you book.

Approach

Orthodontic treatment is long, so the first conversation carries more weight than usual.

You are shown what your bite is doing now, and what the stages would be.

Where a case needs treating, he sets out what the treatment involves. Where waiting and watching is reasonable, he says that out loud instead.

How long treatment runs, and where it finishes, depends on the individual bite. Yours has not been assessed yet.

Where they practise
