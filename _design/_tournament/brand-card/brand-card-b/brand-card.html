02 Palette and roles

Every colour has one job

The navy, the second navy and the yellow are the practice's own, sampled from the live homepage on 2026-07-30 and recorded in C6 section 3. What is new here is the role map, and one genuinely new tone. Sixteen swatches carrying seventeen values, four roles: the light ramp that is the page, the navy that is authority, the yellow that is the offer, and the sage that is reassurance. Three of those values moved in r3 and none of them moved for taste: --ds-ink-soft and --ds-accent-ink were darkened until every text pair on the sheet cleared AAA 7 to 1, and --ds-line-strong was added because the existing hairline was 1.29 to 1 and was being asked to draw input borders.

Lowest text-on-surface ratio anywhere on this sheet: 7.36 : 1, the card body copy sitting on the reassurance wash. That is the AAA bar for small text, not the AA one, and it is the floor rather than the exception: all 27 pairs above clear 7 to 1. The r2 floor was 4.80 : 1 and 193 of 307 painted text elements sat under 7 : 1. Reduced contrast sensitivity, not reduced acuity, is the first thing presbyopia takes, so AAA is the bar this sheet is held to and 2.86 points of the margin above AA is the whole point of the pass.

Where the yellow rules stand, stated plainly. #FCE003 against the ivory card measures 1.31 : 1 and against the canvas 1.24 : 1. That is the honest reason the live site had to deploy it as huge flat bands: at full strength on paper the yellow is almost as luminous as the paper. Every accent rule, cap and underline on this sheet is therefore DECORATIVE emphasis sitting beside text that carries the meaning on its own, which is why WCAG 1.4.11 does not apply to them. The one place the yellow is load-bearing is the offer control, and there it is a fill with 12.84 : 1 navy-deep type on it. The sage marks are held to the 3 : 1 non-text bar instead and clear it: the arc seam reads 3.75 : 1 on the warm wash and 4.09 : 1 on the card.

Where the LINES stand, which r3 had to separate. The two roles were being served by one token and the weaker of them was failing. A decorative panel edge sits on a surface already separated by elevation, so #D9E1EF at 1.29 : 1 is a legitimate drawn hairline and keeps that job. A UI-component boundary is different: WCAG 1.4.11 puts a hard 3 : 1 bar on the edge of an input, a select or an outline control, because that edge is the only thing telling a user where the control is. #D9E1EF was drawing those too. The new --ds-line-strong #6A7DA0 measures 3.89 : 1 on the canvas, 4.09 : 1 on the card, 3.75 : 1 on the warm wash and 3.67 : 1 on the calm wash, and in the dark flip it composites to #7C808B for 4.32 : 1. It also carries the hours rows, which are not a WCAG obligation but are the rule an eye with reduced contrast sensitivity actually tracks a Tuesday along.

Why sage, and the proof that it is new

Every distinct colour in the live homepage snapshot was extracted, in all three notations it uses (six-digit hex, three-digit hex and rgb / rgba). Fifty-five distinct colours were found. #6E8268 is not one of them, and its nearest neighbour by CIEDE2000 among all fifty-five is #7A7A7A, a pure neutral grey, at a distance of 14.8. The just-noticeable difference for CIEDE2000 is about 2.3, so nothing in the live palette is even close.

The same-family test against the colours that are actually the brand: sage sits 117 degrees of hue and 42.3 CIEDE2000 from the navy #17274F, 118 degrees and 41.9 from the second navy #1D2F61, and 53 degrees and 38.6 from the yellow #FCE003. The tone it replaces, #E7B84D, sat only 11 degrees of hue from that yellow, which is why it was worth replacing: it was the accent again at lower saturation, not a relationship.

The live markup does contain four greens and teals, #7bdcb5, #00d084, #009688 and #61ce70, appearing once each. They are the WordPress block-editor default swatches, present in the stylesheet and used by nothing on the page. Sage is not in their family either: all four are 53 to 100 percent saturated at hues 128 to 174, while sage is 11 percent saturated at hue 106. The closest of them, #009688, is 16.5 CIEDE2000 away.

And the reason it is sage rather than any other novel hue: COLOR-THEORY-PRESETS.md entry #1 "Warm Cream + Terracotta" is the catalogue's warm-cream family, its bg-surface is #FBF6EE, one unit from this concept's canvas, and its optional third colour is #6E8268 exactly. BRAND-TOKEN-SCHEMA.md defines --ds-signature as the "Optional 3rd color for personality flourish". The catalogue's own answer for what sits on warm cream is the answer this sheet took, and the role it took it into is the role the schema names.

Retired from the record: #000000, #ffffff, #f6f6f6, #c4c4c4, #d6d6d6 and the concept's own #E7B84D. Pure black and pure white are gone as ink, canvas, rule, scrim and shadow. The three Elementor greys are replaced by the navy-tinted ink and hairline above, per the tint-never-pure rule in the pattern library; the hairline #D9E1EF sits 7.0 CIEDE2000 from #d6d6d6, the nearest of the three, at hue 218 and 41 percent saturation against their 0 percent. #E7B84D is replaced by the sage above.

03 Type

Roboto Slab for warmth, Roboto for clarity

Both families are already self-hosted on the live site. This concept gives them the separation C6 section 4 says is missing: the slab carries every heading, the sans carries every sentence, and Poppins is retired. Numerals move to a tabular mono face so phone numbers, hours and prices read as data, not prose.

The whole ramp moved up one step in r3, together. Display went from a 68px cap to 74px at the same time body went 17px to 18px, so the display-to-body ratio is 4.11 where it was 4.00 and the hierarchy a reader navigates by is untouched. That is the difference between a legible sheet and a large-print one: the large-print version raises body copy and leaves the headline alone, which flattens the page and makes it read as an accommodation. Raising the whole scale and paying for it in whitespace reads as editorial confidence instead, which is what a 25-year-old and a 55-year-old both want to see from a dental practice.

Presbyopia typically begins around 40 and costs both acuity and contrast sensitivity. Motif rules
  • The arc is an elliptical bottom radius on the wash band, and its line is the sage at 2px. That elliptical value is the signature SHAPE, not a fourth entry in the radius scale. Two to four uses per page, one family only.
  • The medallion is always a real photograph of a named clinician, ringed in the card surface with a 1px sage line so it reads as lifted off the seam.
  • Where no consented photograph exists the block ships a flagged placeholder, never a stock substitute. C7 section 3.
  • The 64px accent rule below is the seam's quiet form, used where a portrait would be too heavy. It is the yellow's only appearance inside the motif.

06 One applied moment

The identity doing a job

A single office card. It is the smallest surface where the palette, the type pairing, the radius language and the offer treatment all have to work together at once, and it is where both demotions are easiest to see: the navy that used to fill the header is now a 3px cap and a heading, and the yellow that used to fill the offer band is now a 3px edge on a pale wash.

It is also where r3 pushes hardest, because this is the card made of the three things C7 section 4 calls load-bearing UI. The phone number was a 14px value in a hairline list row, indistinguishable from the opening hours and not tappable at all; it is now the largest element in the card body and a real tel: control with a 56px box. The address went from 13px secondary ink to 17px primary ink at 15.61 to 1. The hours rows went from 14px on a 1.29 to 1 hairline to 17px on a 3.89 to 1 rule, which is what lets an eye track Tuesday across to 7 PM without re-reading the row. The card is the same card; it simply stopped whispering the facts a patient came for.

Standing rules this concept carries

  • Light canvas, navy anchor. The dark ground is punctuation, matching the measured dark_ratio of 0.154, not a tone pivot.
  • Navy is ink and structure first: headings, hairlines, table rules, edge caps and the primary control. It fills a background only where a reversed lockup has to be shown.
  • One accent. Yellow means the published offer and nothing else, which is how it already behaves on the live site, and it is deployed as rules, caps and marks with exactly one full-strength moment. No flat yellow band appears anywhere on this sheet.
  • One third tone. Sage carries reassurance, never the offer and never authority, so the three registers stay separable at a glance.
  • No pure black and no pure white survive as ink, canvas, rule, scrim or shadow, and none of the three Elementor greys survives at all.
  • Every superlative is replaced by a specific: a named credential, a real review count, a stated opening hour. Nothing on this sheet is invented.
  • No countdown timers, no artificial urgency, no badge walls, no stock smile close-ups. C7 section 2.
  • Legibility is a floor, not a preference, because anxious and older patients are a named audience and presbyopia costs contrast sensitivity as much as acuity. C7 section 4. Body is 18px at 1.65 in weight 400 with zero tracking; nothing anywhere on the sheet is set below 14px; every text-on-surface pair clears AAA 7 to 1, not merely AA; every control's rendered box is at least 48px against the WCAG 2.5.5 bar of 44; and negative tracking exists on the display face alone.
  • Hours, addresses and phone numbers are treated as the load-bearing UI C7 section 4 says they are: every phone number is a tel: control sized as a control, the address is set in the primary ink, and the hours rows sit on a rule that clears 3 to 1 rather than a hairline that does not.
  • None of that was bought by shrinking the design. The display ramp rose with the body ramp, the extra size is paid for in whitespace rather than density, and the composition, the arc seam motif, the restrained motion and the slab-over-sans pairing are untouched.
  • Open owner items visible here: no vector logo master (C6 section 2), and no published offer terms (C3 pricing).
