This concept departs from the C7-locked colour relationship

What C7 locked. visual-direction.md section 48 states: "the existing deep navy carries authority and the yellow is the accent that already signals the offer. Keep that relationship; the build decides how." C7 gave the build freedom over deployment, not over the relationship itself.

What this sheet does. The owner-supplied palette removes the brand navy #17274F entirely and makes deep teal #0B3D3A the authority colour. The accent #FFD447 is close to, but not the same as, the brand yellow #FCE003. Both halves of the locked pair have changed, so this is a change to the relationship, not to its deployment.

Consequence. The owner directed the palette and it is applied here in full. C7 is now out of date with respect to this concept and requires an upstream re-lock in prep, SKILL.md P7.75, before concept D can advance to stage (2) CHROME. This sheet does not argue for or against the change. It records it.

Move 01

Teal carries the separation

D used to separate from the live site through warm paper. The owner's canvas is cool, and it sits only 1.05:1 away from the live site's white, so the paper can no longer do that job. Teal now carries it: the ink, the rules, the chips, the arcs and both dark grounds are teal, which the live site has nowhere at any strength.

Move 02

The accent gets a keyline

#FFD447 measures 1.36:1 on this canvas, so a bare yellow mark is a ghost. Every yellow mark on a light ground now carries a 1px deep-teal keyline. The yellow keeps its job and the teal makes it visible.

Move 03

Two teals, two jobs

The owner's #128C7E cannot carry text in either direction, so it is a boundary and graphic colour only. A derived step, #0D504A, is the one teal legal as text on light and as a fill under light text, at 8.84:1 and 8.21:1. Deep teal #0B3D3A stays the ink and the ground.

Lockups

Wordmark, mark, reversal

Built as type plus a drawn mark because no vector master exists. The mark is a reductive arc: the smile line the practice already owns, cut to a single stroke so it survives a favicon. On light grounds the yellow segment is drawn over a deep-teal stroke, which is the keyline rule applied to the mark itself.

Radiant SmilesDental Care

The mark and the descriptor both grew in this pass: the descriptor used to set at 10.5px, which is below the 14px floor this sheet now holds everywhere. The yellow segment sits on a 5.2px deep-teal stroke, giving it a 0.8px keyline on each side.

Palette

Four owner hexes, and the steps they force

The four hexes below are owner-supplied and carried verbatim. They are not enough on their own to build a compliant system, because two of the four cannot carry text at all. Every derived step is listed underneath with the measurement that forced it.

Owner canvas #F7FAFA

The canvas. Cool near-white, hue 180. Replaces concept D's warm ivory #F6F3EC. Still not #ffffff.

Owner primary-deep #0B3D3A

Ink and authority. Headings, keylines, both dark grounds. Replaces the brand navy #17274F, which is gone.

Owner signature #128C7E

The signature teal. Boundaries, keylines, arcs and graphics only. It cannot carry text in either direction. See the forbidden rows below.

Owner accent #FFD447

Decorative only. Never a foreground anywhere. Legal as a ground in exactly one case: under deep teal, at 8.47:1.

primary, derived #0D504A

The one teal legal in both directions. Eyebrows, labels, links and the primary button fill. Moved deeper in this pass: the old step #10746A cleared AA at 5.37:1 but not the 7:1 floor a 40-plus audience needs. This one reads 8.84:1.

ink, derived #09302D

Body ink, teal-tinted near-black. Replaces the live #000000.

ink-soft, derived #23504D

Secondary ink, ledes, captions and the field placeholder. A desaturated teal, not a generic grey. Also moved deeper: the old #3F6764 sat at 6.00:1, which is AA but not AAA. This one reads 8.60:1 and still steps back from the body ink.

bg-surface, derived #EDF5F4

The card. It steps DOWN from the canvas, not up, because the canvas is already at 97% lightness.

bg-band, derived #E0EFEE

The band. Replaces the live #f6f6f6 alternating band with a colour that belongs to the brand.

primary-soft, derived #D2E8E6

The teal-on-teal solve. Deep teal reads 9.42:1 here, against 2.91:1 on the signature teal.

line-control, derived #547674

Control boundaries: input borders, outline buttons. Was #698987 at 3.62:1, the bare non-text minimum. Now 4.74:1 and drawn at 2px, because a hairline that only just clears 3:1 is the first edge a 40-plus eye stops finding.

line, derived #CDD8D7

Decorative hairline at 1.39:1. Separation only. Never a control boundary and never a text pairing.

  • Derived step 01

    #0D504A, deep teal 24% into the signature teal. It exists because the owner's #128C7E reads 3.94:1 as text on the canvas and 3.94:1 under near-white text, so it fails 4.5:1 in both directions. The first revision answered that with #10746A at 5.37:1, which clears AA and nothing more. Against a 40-plus primary audience the bar is AAA, so the step moved deeper: #0D504A reads 8.84:1 as text on canvas and carries near-white at 8.21:1. One step, both jobs, both AAA.

  • Derived step 02

    #D2E8E6, signature teal 16% into the canvas. This is the answer to the 2.91:1 teal-on-teal problem. Deep teal is never set on the signature teal anywhere in this system; where a teal ground has to hold teal type, it is this tint, measured at 9.42:1.

  • Derived step 03

    #EDF5F4 card and #E0EFEE band, signature teal at 4.5% and 10% into the canvas. Cards step DOWN in lightness rather than up, because the owner canvas is already at 97% and any lighter card would land on white, which concept D excludes.

  • Derived step 04

    #09302D ink and #23504D ink-soft, black 22% into the deep teal and deep teal 10% into the canvas. Body ink at 13.59:1 and secondary at 8.60:1, so the sheet never leans on the 11.48:1 heading colour for long-form reading and the secondary tier is still AAA. The previous ink-soft, #3F6764, sat at 6.00:1: legal, but the exact band reduced contrast sensitivity punishes first.

  • Derived step 05

    #547674, deep teal 38% into the canvas. Control boundaries need 3:1 and the decorative hairline #CDD8D7 only reaches 1.39:1. Every input border and outline button uses this instead, at 4.74:1 on canvas and 4.49:1 on the card, drawn at 2px rather than 1px.

  • Derived step 06

    #0B423E, signature teal 6% into the deep teal, as the lighter stop of both dark grounds. The radial teal overlay came down from 30% to 10% at the same time, because at 30% the worst composite was #0F6159 and the accent on it measured only 5.14:1. The worst composite is now #0C4944, which holds on-dark text at 9.05:1 and the accent at 7.19:1. Both AAA.

  • Derived step 07

    No alpha on any text, anywhere. The old sheet faded on-dark runs to 82% and 86%, which composited to 4.94:1 and 5.26:1. Faded type is the first thing an ageing eye loses, so every on-dark run is now solid #E9F3F3. The only surviving alphas on this sheet are borders, washes and shadows, none of which carry a letter.

Every text-on-surface and boundary pairing that ships on this sheet, recomputed from scratch with the WCAG 2.1 relative luminance formula. Type

Two families doing two jobs, at a size that holds

The live site loads Poppins, Roboto and Roboto Slab with no separation of display and body use. Concept D keeps two of the three and gives each a single job. Poppins is dropped. Roboto Mono carries figures only, so prices, hours and ratings read as data. The families are unchanged by the palette revision, because C6 records them and the owner changed only the colours. What did change is the whole scale. C7 section 4 names anxious and older patients as an audience and calls for legible type, real contrast and tap targets that work; the ADA Health Policy Institute figure behind that, 55 percent of adults aged 65 and over with no dental benefits, is the reason the practice publishes a senior price at all. Presbyopia typically begins around 40 and costs both acuity and contrast sensitivity. So the ramp below is built to measured floors rather than to taste.

  • Floor 01, size 18 / 14

    Body sets at 18px and NOTHING on this sheet renders below 14px. The previous revision ran a 9.5px owner badge, 10px asset captions and 10.5px table headers. All of them moved.

  • Floor 02, leading 1.70

    Body line-height is 1.70 and no reading run drops under 1.60. Leading is the cheapest legibility gain available and the one that reads editorial rather than clinical.

  • Floor 03, contrast 7:1

    AAA, not AA. Every text pairing in the table above clears 7:1, and the lowest that ships is 7.06:1. Four palette steps moved to get there rather than one.

  • Floor 04, weight 400

    No 300, 200 or 100 anywhere text has to be read. Thin weights are the first thing to collapse for an older eye, and the display face carries emphasis at 500 to 700 instead.

  • Floor 05, tracking 0.00em

    No negative tracking at reading size. Negative tracking survives only on the slab display face at 27px and up, where it is an optical correction. The 21px display step is zeroed.

  • Floor 06, targets 44 px

    Every control clears 44 by 44 CSS px measured in render, not inferred from padding. The 13 controls measure 52.6 to 55.6px tall for buttons, 59.2px for the field, and 204.4 by 48px for each of the 8 phone numbers, which is the smallest target on the sheet.

Atoms

Controls, tags, fields, rows

Radius is three roles only: control 8px, surface 14px, pill full. The focus ring changed with the palette. The previous revision used the yellow at full strength on the argument that a warning colour has to be unmissable. On this canvas the yellow measures 1.36:1 and an unmissable ring would have been an invisible one, so focus is now a deep-teal outline at 11.48:1 with a teal halo.

Buttons

Solid on the derived teal, outline on the canvas, quiet as a text action whose underline is the lead rule itself. The solid fill is #0D504A and not the owner's #128C7E, because near-white on #128C7E measures 3.94:1. Every one of the three is at least 52px tall, measured in render, against the 44px WCAG 2.5.5 AAA bar.

What the per-glyph method caught that the line-box method missed. Two defects passed the previous, coarser pass. First, the masthead lede actually measured 6.75:1 at 768 while the sheet claimed 7.50:1, so the most-read run on the page was under its own floor; the grid and wash were cut (see the token notes) and it now reads 7.56:1. Second, the E5 swipe drew its 2px deep-teal rule on the bottom edge of the inline content box, exactly where a Roboto descender lands: the "p" of "per office" put one glyph pixel on the rule at 1.18:1 and that descender tip vanished. Only the words WITH descenders were affected, which is why words like "force" and "holds" measured 9.15:1 and hid it. A 0.15em bottom pad cleared the rule and that run now measures 10.85:1.
