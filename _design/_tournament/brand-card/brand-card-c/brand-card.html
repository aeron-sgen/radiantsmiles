02
Colour

Palette and the role each colour plays

Four owner hexes, one warm family, hue 28.6 to 41.7. The ink carries authority and is the only thing on this sheet that draws a boundary the gold cannot. The gold is decorative by measurement, not by preference: at 1.71 to 1 on the canvas there is no light ground it can sit on legibly, so it is deployed only as a cut inside an ink block. White and black are not used raw. Every derived step below is named, justified and traceable to one of the four.

Type

One loud face, one quiet face, one for numbers

Barlow Condensed carries the display sizes and nothing else. Roboto, which this practice already self-hosts, stays as the body. C7 section 4 names anxious and OLDER patients as an audience, so the body is now set at 18px on a 1.7 leading with a hard 14px floor under everything on the sheet, and no reading weight below 400. Roboto Mono takes the hours, phone numbers, prices and review counts, which are the specifics that replace the adjectives being removed. The display-to-body ratio is deliberately HELD at about 7 to 1: the display scale went up with the body, so the sheet gained legibility without losing the scale jump that is where most of concept C's boldness lives. Larger type on a disciplined grid reads editorial, not large-print.

04
Components

Buttons, cards, tags, inputs

One radius dialect throughout: controls at 4px, surfaces at 10px, the full round reserved for status. Buttons invert rather than fade. There is no gold-filled control on this sheet on either polarity: the offer button is the ink block with the motif's own gold cut at its leading edge, measured 5.42 to 1 against the fill it cuts. Every control on this sheet clears 44 by 44 CSS px, enforced with min-height and measured in render rather than inferred from padding: the smallest one measures 52px. The office card carries the contact rail, where the phone number is a 24px mono numeral inside a real tel: target and the address sits at 18px in full ink rather than in the caption ink, because C7 section 4 calls that data load-bearing UI. Every control label also sits on the sheet's 1.6 leading floor, which is what took the smallest target from 46px to 52px: a control sizes from its line box, so the reading floor and the tap floor turn out to be the same measurement taken twice.

Buttons, the real CTA verbs from C3

Hover inverts fill and outline over 200ms on named properties, never on "all" and never by fading opacity. Focus draws a double ring: a 2px gold keyline hugging the control, then a 3px ink outline. The gold alone is 1.71:1 on the canvas and cannot carry a focus indicator, so the ink carries it at 9.25:1 and the gold stays the cut against that ink at 5.42:1.

Signature

The offer rule

One device, four placements. A hard stop between the ink, the gold and the sand at a fixed 62 / 10 / 28 split. It never blends, never rounds at the join, and never appears twice in one view except as the section rule. It is the offer band this practice already runs, reduced to a mark and re-weighted so the ink carries it. The three slots are bound to their own tokens, not to --ds-primary, so the dark-surface flip cannot turn the 62% block into a flat paper field.
