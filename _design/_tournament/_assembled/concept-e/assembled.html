Welcome 02 / 11

We lead with an address, not with an award.

Radiant Smiles is one dental practice that operates seven of its own offices across Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson. It is not a network, a franchise or a referral service, so the team that treats you is our team wherever you are seen.

Six kinds of care are handled in-house: general, cosmetic, restorative, surgical, periodontic and orthodontic. Same-day and emergency appointments are available.
