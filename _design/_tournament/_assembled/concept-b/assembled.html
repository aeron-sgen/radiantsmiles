Why this practice 04 / 10

One practice. One clinical team. Seven front doors.

A group name on seven signs does not always mean one practice behind them. Here it does.

  • One clinical team at every location, so your record and your plan travel with you.
  • Six specialties in house, from a routine exam through to orthodontic treatment.
  • Hours published per office. Four of the seven are closed Saturday, and we say so.

Radiant Smiles is a single dental practice that operates seven of its own offices, employing named licensed clinicians who deliver care in house across six specialties. Not a network, not a franchise, not a referral service.

Proof and receipts 08 / 10

Proof, with the receipt attached to it.

In 2023, 53 percent of adults with private dental insurance had at least one dental visit, against 16 percent of those with no dental insurance. American Dental Association Health Policy Institute, Coverage Access and Outcomes, fetched 2026-07-30.

That is the whole reason this practice takes insurance at seven addresses and publishes a $45 entry visit. Coverage is the difference between a patient who comes in and a patient who waits until it hurts.

Twenty one percent of adults aged 19 to 64 have no dental benefits, and 55 percent of adults aged 65 and over have none at all. Both figures come from the same American Dental Association source cited above.
