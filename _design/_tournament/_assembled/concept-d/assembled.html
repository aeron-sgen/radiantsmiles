Stage 3 of 8

Why this practice, in plain terms.

A lot of dental groups are one shared name over separately run offices. We are not built that way, and it changes what you can expect from us.

  1. 1

    One practice, not a network.

    Radiant Smiles is a single dental practice that operates seven of its own offices. It is not a network, a franchise or a referral service.

  2. 2

    Six kinds of care in house.

    General, cosmetic, restorative, surgical, periodontic and orthodontic care are delivered by our own licensed clinicians.

  3. 3

    Seven neighbourhoods, close to home.

    Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson. Every address and phone number on this page is first party captured, office by office.

We are not going to tell you we are the best in Las Vegas. We are going to tell you where we are, what we do, and what it costs.

The rule this whole site is written to.
