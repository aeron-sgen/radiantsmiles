## §2 - pain-it-kills  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

Most people who grind their teeth do it while asleep. You do not feel it happening. What you notice is a sore jaw in the morning. Sometimes a dull headache at the temple, or a tooth that has started to feel flat.

Grinding can wear enamel down quietly. Enamel does not grow back. By the time a tooth chips or a filling cracks, the repair is a larger one.

That is the whole reason to deal with it early. Not because grinding is an emergency, but because it is slow, silent and cumulative.

Coverage is the other half of the picture. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly, per the same source.
