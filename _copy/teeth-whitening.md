## §2 - pain-it-kills  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

If you have tried a pharmacy kit, you know the pattern. Strips and pens lift some of the surface stain, and then they stop. That plateau is where a kit reaches its limit.

Patchiness is the other limit. A flat strip sits against an uneven arch, so the front surfaces lighten and the edges do not. Crowns, veneers and fillings do not change shade at all.

Stain also sits in two places. Some of it is on the surface. Some of it is deeper in the tooth, where a pharmacy kit cannot reach.

Coverage is worth being plain about. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly. Those figures describe dental coverage and dental visits generally, not whitening.

**What does whitening cost?**
We quote treatment after an examination, because the plan depends on what your teeth are actually doing. We do not publish treatment prices on this site.

Coverage depends on your plan. Our insurance and financing page publishes the full list of named plans. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

There is no drilling and no injection. Some patients may experience tooth sensitivity afterwards, as our own FAQ page states. Tell us if that happens, because there are things we can do about it.
