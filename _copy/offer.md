## §2 - what-is-included  [M]
required IDs: D-OFFER-017

**The examination.** A dentist looks at your teeth, your gums and your bite, and records what is there today.

**The X-rays.** Digital X-rays are taken to show what an examination on its own cannot see.

**The conversation.** You are told what was found and what we suggest, in plain terms, before anything is booked.

What the price does not include is treatment. Cleanings, fillings, extractions and everything else are separate, and none of them are decided before the exam.

Treatment here runs on quote-only pricing. No treatment price is published on this site, because the number depends on what the examination actually finds.

At the end of the visit you can request a quote for the work we suggest.

## §4 - who-it-is-for  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

This is for new patients. The lower price is the same visit for seniors, at $29 rather than $45.

The reason the senior price exists is a coverage gap, not a promotion. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026.

Dental visits track coverage directly, which is the second finding that same ADA source records.

A stated price on the first visit means the cost of that visit is known before you book it.
