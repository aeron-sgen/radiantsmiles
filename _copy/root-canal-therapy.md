## §2 - pain-it-kills  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

The nerve inside a tooth sits in a sealed space. When it becomes infected, the pressure has nowhere to go. That is why the pain can be severe. Pain that throbs or keeps you awake is a reason to ask for an urgent appointment.

It does not settle by itself. It can quieten, which people read as recovery. The infection is still there when it comes back.

By then the choice may be narrower. Treating the tooth early is a different conversation from treating it after an abscess.

Cost is part of why people ride it out. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly.

## §3 - benefit-pillars  [M x3]
required IDs: D-POSITION-009

**Deal with the source of the pain.** The treatment removes the infected tissue from inside the tooth. That is the thing generating the pressure.

**Keep your own tooth in your own jaw.** A treated tooth stays in place and keeps doing its job. An extraction is followed by a discussion about replacement choices such as a bridge, a denture or a dental implant.

**Be seen while it is still today.** Same-day and emergency appointments are available at our offices. Call the office nearest you and say that it is painful.

## §8 - faq  [S each]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-009

**What does a root canal cost?**
It is quoted after the examination, because the tooth and the number of canals decide the work. We do not publish treatment prices. Any figure given before the X-ray would only change after it.

**Does insurance cover it?**
Coverage depends on your plan, and on how the treatment and the crown are coded. Our insurance and financing page publishes the full list of named plans. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

**Does it hurt?**
We will not tell you the treatment is painless. The tooth is numbed before anything starts. If you feel anything sharp during it, tell us and we stop.

**What happens if I wait?**
The infection does not resolve on its own, and the options can narrow over time. If you are in pain now, call the nearest office rather than sitting with it.
