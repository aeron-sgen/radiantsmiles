## §2 - pain-it-kills  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

Enamel weakens long before a cavity is visible. The surface loses minerals first, and nothing about that stage looks or feels like a problem.

You cannot see it in a mirror. It is the quiet part of decay, the months or years before there is a hole to fill.

Fluoride is aimed at that window. It is not a cure for a cavity, and we will not describe it as one. It is help for a surface that is losing ground.

The published coverage numbers belong here, stated plainly. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly in that same source. In 2023, 53% of privately insured adults had a visit, against 16% of those with no dental insurance.
