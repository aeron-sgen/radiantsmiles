## §2 - pain-it-kills  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

Early changes in the mouth may not be readily noticeable. A white or red patch. A sore that has not closed up. A lump on the side of the tongue you cannot see in a mirror.

None of that hurts at the start. That is exactly why it tends to be found by someone who is looking for it, rather than by the person it belongs to.

Few of us examine the floor of our own mouth or the underside of our own tongue. A clinician does, at every examination, with a light and gloved hands.

A screening only helps if you are in the chair. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly, so people without benefits are seen less often.

## §4 - capability-breakdown  [M each]
required IDs: D-OFFER-017

**We ask first.** How long the spot has been there, whether it has changed, and what your history is. Tobacco and alcohol use both go on the record, because they matter here.

**We look and we feel.** Lips, cheeks, gums, palate, the sides and underside of the tongue, the floor of the mouth, and the glands in your neck.

**We do not rely on the X-ray for this.** Our own digital X-ray page publishes what they may reveal. This part of the check is done by eye and by hand.

**We tell you what happens next.** If something needs a closer look, we say so, and we say what the next step is. We do not diagnose from a glance.
