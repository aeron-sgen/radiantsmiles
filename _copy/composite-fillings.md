## §2 - pain-it-kills  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

A cavity does not heal. It is a hole in the enamel that gets wider and deeper while you carry on eating. Early on it is a small repair.

Left long enough, decay reaches the nerve. At that point the conversation is about root canal treatment or an extraction, not a filling. Same tooth, much bigger job.

The other thing people mention is how a repair looks. A metal filling shows every time you laugh. A composite is shaded to the tooth it sits in.

Coverage is the other half of the picture. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly, per the same source.

## §3 - benefit-pillars  [M x3]
required IDs: D-POSITION-009

**Laugh without a flash of metal.** The repair is shaded to the tooth beside it, so it is not the first thing people see.

**Keep more of your own tooth.** Composite bonds to the tooth structure, so the preparation can stay conservative rather than being cut wide to hold a filling in.

**Deal with it instead of watching it.** The decayed part is removed and the tooth is rebuilt, rather than left to get wider.

## §4 - capability-breakdown  [M each]
required IDs: D-OFFER-017

**We find it.** Decay between teeth and under an old filling is not visible to you. Digital X-rays show what a mirror cannot.
*Image slot:* a digital X-ray on the operatory screen, with the clinician pointing at the area being discussed.

**We rebuild the tooth.** Composite is placed in layers, cured, then shaped back to the form of the tooth. The shade is matched to the tooth next to it.

**We check the bite.** You are asked to bite down and say how it feels. A filling that sits high is adjusted before you leave the chair.
