## §2 - pain-it-kills  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

Recession is easier to feel with cold water than to see in a mirror. One tooth flinches, and then it does it again.

What has happened is that the gum has moved back and left part of the root uncovered. Root surface is softer than enamel.

It is more sensitive, it decays faster, and it collects plaque more easily. The gum does not creep back over it on its own.

Recession also often moves slowly, which is exactly what makes it easy to ignore. Change that gradual can be hard to see in a bathroom mirror.

Money is the other reason it goes unwatched. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. The same source reports that dental visits track coverage directly.

## §3 - benefit-pillars  [M x3]
required IDs: D-POSITION-009

**Drink something cold without bracing.** Sensitivity is the symptom people actually want gone. Treating the exposed root is what addresses it.

**Change the one thing that is causing it.** Hard brushing, grinding and gum disease are treated differently. Knowing which one it is changes what you do at home.

**Know at the next visit whether it moved.** The margin is measured and recorded at each visit, so you compare figures rather than memories.

## §8 - faq  [S each]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-009

**What does treating gum recession cost?**
It depends on the cause and on how many teeth are affected. We quote after the examination and the charting. We do not publish treatment prices.

**Does insurance cover it?**
Coverage depends on your plan and on the procedure. Named plans are listed in full on our insurance and financing page. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

**Does treatment hurt?**
The area is numbed before any work begins. Exposed roots can be tender afterwards. We will not promise comfort we cannot control.

**Will my gums grow back?**
Gum tissue does not return over the root by itself. The aim is to stop the recession going further and to protect what is exposed.
