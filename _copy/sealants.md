## §2 - pain-it-kills  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

Look closely at a back tooth. The chewing surface is not flat. It is a set of narrow grooves, and food can become trapped in them.

A brush does not reach the bottom of a narrow groove. So the one spot that collects the most is also the spot a brush cleans least well.

It is why a first cavity can turn up in a molar even in a child who brushes. It is a shape problem before it is a habit problem.

Access decides the rest. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly.

## §3 - benefit-pillars  [M x3]
required IDs: D-POSITION-009

**Cover the groove before it becomes a filling.** A sealed surface has no deep channel left for food to pack into.

**Get through the appointment without drama.** Nothing is drilled and nothing is numbed, which matters if your child is already nervous.

**Have it checked at every visit after that.** A sealant can chip or wear, so we look at it whenever you come in.

## §4 - capability-breakdown  [M each]
required IDs: D-OFFER-017

**We check the tooth first.** A sealant goes over a sound surface. If there is already decay in the groove, sealing it is the wrong move.
*Image slot:* a real photograph of a clinician checking a molar chewing surface in one of our operatories. Not stock.

**The tooth is cleaned and dried.** The coating only bonds to a clean, dry surface, so this step is done carefully.
*Image slot:* a real photograph of the isolation and drying setup used in our own operatory. Not stock.

**The coating is flowed into the grooves.** It runs into the narrow channels and fills them, so the chewing surface becomes smooth and easier to brush.
*Image slot:* a real close-up photograph of a sealed molar taken at one of our offices. Not stock.

**It is set before you leave.** Our own sealants page says the material either hardens immediately or is cured with a special light.
*Image slot:* a real photograph of the curing light in use at one of our operatories. Not stock.
