## §2 - pain-it-kills  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

Losing several teeth changes three things at once. Eating narrows to what you can manage. Certain sounds stop coming out cleanly. And the lower face loses some of the support that used to hold its shape.

People adapt around all of it quietly. Meals get chosen by texture. Photographs get avoided. It rarely arrives as a crisis, which is exactly why it goes unaddressed.

The published coverage numbers belong here too. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. The same source reports that dental visits track coverage directly. In 2023, 53% of adults with private dental insurance had a dental visit, against 16% of adults with no dental insurance.

Those are the figures as published. We state them without stretching them into a claim about who needs dentures.

## §3 - benefit-pillars  [M x3]
required IDs: D-POSITION-009

**Put more food back on the list.** A denture fitted to your ridge and bite gives you a surface to chew against. What you can eat should widen again.

**Have something to speak against again.** Missing teeth change how certain consonants sound. A fitted denture gives the tongue and the lip a surface to work against.

**Get the adjustments without a second appointment somewhere else.** New dentures are adjusted. That is normal, not a fault, and it happens here.

## §4 - capability-breakdown  [M each]
required IDs: D-OFFER-017

**We start with what is still there.** A clinician examines the remaining teeth, the gums and the ridge. Digital X-rays are taken where they show what the surface does not. Full or partial is decided from that.
*Visual:* a real photograph of one of our clinicians examining a patient in our own operatory.

**We take impressions.** The denture is built to your mouth. A partial is also built around the teeth you keep, so those teeth carry the clasps without being loaded unfairly.
*Visual:* a real photograph of an impression tray on our own bench, taken in one of our offices.

**We try it in before it is finished.** You see the shape and the tooth position while they can still be changed. Say what you do not like at that stage. Our own dentures page publishes that the process takes several appointments, and that try-in appointments may be needed, captured 30 July 2026.
*Visual:* a real photograph of a try-in on the bench, with the tooth position still adjustable.

**We fit it and then adjust it.** Sore spots are expected. You come back, we relieve the spot, you carry on. This is part of the treatment, not a sign it went wrong.
*Visual:* a real photograph of a denture mid-adjustment in one of our own rooms.

**We keep checking the fit.** Ridges change shape and a denture that fits now may not later. Relines and repairs are handled here.
*Visual:* a real photograph of the chair where a fit check and a reline are carried out.

## §8 - faq  [S each]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-009

**What do dentures cost?**
We quote after the examination, because a partial for two gaps and a full upper are different pieces of work. We do not publish treatment prices. A number given first would only change later.

**Does insurance cover them?**
Coverage depends on your plan. Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife and the Culinary Health Fund are among the 41 carriers listed on our insurance and financing page. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

⚠OWNER: this answer names carriers without stating any relationship with them, per `client-rules.json` OVERCLAIM.global[5], which records the site-wide network claim as unconfirmed. It offers no coverage check made on your behalf, because that activity is undefined and blocked under `content-bible.md` §2, per `intake-brief.md` §G-6. Confirm the per-office network status before this answer ships.

**Does getting them fitted hurt?**
The impressions do not. If teeth have to come out first, that is done with anaesthetic and the site is given time to heal. Early sore spots are adjusted out.

**Will they slip?**
Fit is what controls that, and fit is what the adjustment visits are for. Tell us where it moves and we work on it. We will not promise you a result we cannot see yet.
