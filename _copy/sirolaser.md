When people hear the words gum surgery, they picture a scalpel. Cutting, stitches, swelling and time off work.

That picture is why gum treatment gets postponed. The appointment feels like something to brace for rather than book.

So the pocket stays. The infection keeps working under the gumline while the decision waits for a better time.

Cost stacks on top of the dread. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly.

We would rather tell you exactly which instrument we use, and let you decide with the facts in front of you.

**What does laser assisted gum treatment cost?**
We quote once your gums have been examined, because the number of sites treated drives the work. We do not publish treatment prices.

**Does insurance cover it?**
Coverage depends on your plan and on how the treatment is coded. Our insurance and financing page publishes the full list of named plans. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

**Does it hurt?**
The area is numbed where numbing is needed. We will not tell you the procedure is painless, and we will not promise a recovery time we have not published.

**Is a laser better than surgery?**
That is a clinical judgement for your specific pockets, not a slogan. We will explain why one is being recommended for you, and what the alternative is.
