## §2 - pain-it-kills  [M]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-007

Plenty of adults want their teeth straighter and put it off anyway. The picture in their head is brackets, wires and looking fifteen again in a work meeting.

So it waits. Meanwhile the crowded spot stays hard to floss. The tooth that sits out of line keeps taking more load than it should.

Aligners change the trade. They are clear, they come out for meals, and you can brush and floss the way you already do.

Cost is the other reason it waits. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 21% of adults aged 19 to 64 have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. The same source records 55% for adults aged 65 and older, and finds that dental visits track coverage directly.

## §8 - faq  [S each]
required IDs: D-INCUMBENT-009

**What does Invisalign cost?**
We quote after records are taken, because the fee follows the number of aligners your case needs. We do not publish treatment prices, and we will not guess at one.

**Will my insurance cover it?**
Coverage depends on your plan and on how orthodontic care is coded. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

⚠OWNER: no benefits-checking step is published anywhere on the live site. Confirm what the practice actually does before this answer describes a process.

**Does it hurt?**
Each new aligner feels tight going in, because that is the tray moving the teeth. There are no brackets or wires being tightened, and no needle is involved.
